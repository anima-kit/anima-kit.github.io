# Information Extraction with Docling and NuMind

I tried out [Docling][docling] + [NuMind][numind] for information extraction from unstructured data. :material-flask-round-bottom:{.md-icon}

<!-- more -->

And it's pretty powerful right out of the box :material-generator-portable:{.md-icon}. It was insanely easy to setup and use - and it worked well for a lot of various use cases without much guidance or customization. 

To see how to use it for your own use cases, you can checkout the [official tutorial from Docling][docling-tutorial]. I also show how to use this tool for extracting key information from receipts using a [Kaggle dataset][kaggle-dataset]. You can checkout the tutorial [here][docling-tutorial-ak] and [here][medium-post].

In the near future, I plan on diving into more of the Docling modules to see how much control I can get over my document extraction pipelines :material-flask-round-bottom:{.md-icon}. Stay tuned for more posts! :material-tune-vertical:{.md-icon}. 

You can also [checkout my current projects][projects] :material-rocket-launch-outline:{.md-icon} or dive into [in-depth tutorials][tutorials] covering various aspects of AI :material-bookshelf:{.md-icon}.
